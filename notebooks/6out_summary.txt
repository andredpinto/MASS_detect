## 1. What the data is (6outMini cell 0; 6out Part 1/3)

| Dataset | Contents | How they use it |
|---|---|---|
| LNDb train | 236 CT scans, 1219 unique findings (768 nodules), radiologist masks for nodules >= 3 mm | Main training data |
| LNDb test | 58 CT scans, 2900 candidates (50 per CT), centroid only | Inference only — every candidate predicted as if it were a nodule |
| LIDC-IDRI | 1010 patients, 1018 CT series, up to 4 readings per nodule | Extra training data (mainly the size model) |

Files kept: trainNodules_gt.csv (merged findings — the unit of analysis is the nodule,
not the reading, to avoid leakage), chars_trainNodules.csv (malignancy ratings),
LNDbAcqParams.csv (scanner info), radiologist masks, official trainFolds.csv.
Dropped: LIDC per-patient diagnosis (says the patient has cancer but not which nodule),
Fleischner files and reports (follow-up recommendation, not malignancy; absent in test),
.rar archives, LIDC X-ray series, macOS ._ files.

## 2. Pipeline (6out Parts 2-4)

1. **One nodule table** (`nodule_table.csv`, 3882 rows, shared schema for both datasets;
   Part 3). LNDb: 1219 findings / 768 nodules / 765 rated. LIDC: 2663 nodules from
   875 patients. LIDLc groups with >4 annotations (a pylidc weakness) are regrouped by a
   proximity rule (join annotations whose centres are closer than the larger of their radii,
   min 3 mm); 26 rows regrouped, 6 still oversized and flagged in `oversized_group`.
   Label = mean of ratings (0 = "not filled in" -> NaN). Placeholder volumes (3 mm sphere
   stored when only a centre was marked) are flagged and emptied. Lobe matched by position
   (within 3 mm), not by FindingID (90 of 1152 findings would be wrong by ID).
   Label views derived: ternary (<=2.5 benign, <=3.5 indeterminate, else malignant) and binary.
2. **3D patches** (Part 4): 64 mm cube resampled to 1 mm isotropic voxels (64^3), raw HU,
   centre of the finding on voxel (32,32,32), cube axes aligned with world axes (handles
   tilted CTs correctly through SimpleITK; outside the CT filled with -1024 HU and the
   outside fraction recorded). All 1219 train + 2900 test + 2663 LIDC patches cut and saved
   as .npy with manifests. QC: coordinate check against radiologist masks — mask centroid
   within 1 mm of voxel 32 for all 45 checked readings (tilted and untilted); 12 training
   "duplicates" are nodule/non-nodule twins of the same lesion — only one of each pair is
   rated, so labelled nodules never appear twice; 25 train / 47 test patches have >5% of
   voxels outside the CT (flagged); 42 LIDC nodules (1.6%) whose position could not be
   verified by two independent computations are excluded.
3. **Automatic segmentation from the centre only** (Part 5, `segment_from_centre`), needed
   because the test file gives only centroids: (1) clip to [-1024, 400] HU, Gaussian smooth
   sigma 0.7 mm; (2) adaptive threshold halfway between centre HU (median in 2 mm sphere) and
   lung HU (median of < -500 HU voxels in a 24-31 mm shell, else -850), capped at -300 HU;
   if the centre is < 150 HU denser than lung -> empty mask; (3) keep the connected region
   touching the centre (max 28 mm from it); (4) cut at 1.2 x radius of the widest inscribed
   sphere near the centre + 1 mm (removes attached vessels/pleura); (5) keep the connected
   region touching the centre again. Validated on 250 nodules: median Dice 0.64 vs the
   radiologists (who agree better with each other), diameter Spearman 0.72, mean abs diameter
   error ~1.5 mm. 91.9% of rated train nodules segment successfully (8% fail).
4. **Handcrafted features** (30 numbers): size (volume, equivalent diameter, max extent),
   shape (elongation, flatness, sphericity), density (mean/std/p10/p50/p90/skew),
   solid/ground-glass fractions, edge gradient, ring features (mean HU, solid fraction,
   contrast), segmentation diagnostics (centre/lung/threshold HU), plus statistics in spheres
   of 3/5/8 mm around the centre (need no mask, so they exist for every finding).

## 3. PyRadiomics analysis (Part 5.3) — directly relevant to our parallel track

Config: binWidth 25 HU, 1 mm isotropic (resampling done when the patch is cut, so
pyradiomics does not resample again), sitkLinear interpolator, Original + Laplacian-of-Gaussian
with sigma 1/2/3 mm, all feature classes (firstorder, shape, GLCM, GLRLM, GLSZM, GLDM, NGTDM)
= 386 features, ROI = the automatic mask (identical in train and test).

Robustness (ICC(2,1), two-way random, absolute agreement, single rater; robust = ICC >= 0.75):
- Between two radiologists' masks: 330/386 features robust, median ICC 0.94.
- Between the automatic mask and a radiologist mask (632 nodules): median ICC 0.73, only 47%
  of features robust. Conclusion: the weak point is the segmentation, not the features.

**Head-to-head (ordinal model, same grouped CV, 3 repeats):**
- 30 handcrafted features: BA 0.377, macro F1 0.406, QWK 0.501, MAE 0.633, composite 0.651.
- Handcrafted + robust radiomics (|Spearman| > 0.9 filter, keep 30 most related to the rating,
  stronger penalty alpha=10): BA 0.355, F1 0.379, QWK 0.470, MAE 0.644, composite 0.561.
- Size model (predicts log of radiologists' diameter): Spearman 0.828 with handcrafted
  features, 0.822 with radiomics added.
- **Decision: radiomic features do not improve the model; the final model uses the 30
  handcrafted features.** Their stated reason to keep the radiomics analysis in the report:
  evidence, and "a better segmentation would be the way to make radiomics worthwhile".

## 4. Model and validation (Part 6)

- **Ordinal logistic regression** (own implementation, `NoduleMalignancyModel`) on the 30
  features, with a **learned size feature**: a HistGradientBoostingRegressor predicts the log
  of the radiologists' equivalent diameter from the patch features (out-of-fold, GroupKFold(5)
  by CT, stacked like Wolpert 1992; log target so errors are relative; 2.5 mm imputed for
  nodules < 3 mm). This is "learning using privileged information" (Vapnik & Vashist 2009):
  the diameter exists in training but not in test.
- LIDC usage: tested 4 ways, always validating on LNDb only. LIDC in the classifier HURTS
  (composite -0.18 to -0.27 vs LNDb-only, all CIs exclude 0), with or without a dataset
  indicator or ComBat harmonization (reference batch LNDb, one batch per LIDC manufacturer,
  malignancy as biological covariate). **LIDC is used only in the size model** (a physical
  quantity that means the same in both datasets; size model Spearman 0.859 with LIDC).
- Class imbalance correction: probabilities divided by the training class share raised to
  power tau; tau = 0.5 chosen as the compromise (tau=0 plain argmax favours MAE, tau=1 favours
  balanced accuracy).
- Validation: StratifiedGroupKFold(5) by patient/CT, repeated (3-5 seeds), 95% CIs by
  cluster bootstrap over CTs; also confirmed on the official LNDb folds (which played no part
  in any decision) and on new seeds (to avoid selection bias, Cawley & Talbot 2010).
- Results (official folds): final model BA 0.411, macro F1 0.429, QWK 0.533, MAE 0.654;
  v1 (ridge + tuned cut points) BA 0.398, F1 0.384, QWK 0.519, MAE 0.714.
  **Ceiling: radiologist-vs-radiologist agreement is QWK 0.527 (LNDb), 0.466 (LIDC)** —
  the label noise itself is the limit.
- Permutation importance: volume, threshold HU, ring mean HU, hu_std, ring solid fraction
  dominate — size and density carry the signal.
- Submission: `reports/predictions.csv`, 2900 rows (LNDbID, FindingID, x, y, z, prediction),
  model trained on all 765 LNDb nodules + 2621 LIDC nodules (size model only). Prediction
  distribution: 1: 5%, 2: 29%, 3: 48%, 4: 17%, 5: 0.6%.

## 5. Things to keep in mind when reviewing

- The notebook asserts things we should not silently copy: e.g. it uses `nodule_table.csv`,
  `patches/`, `reports/` artifacts that exist on the author's machine, not in this checkout.
- `chars_trainNodules.csv` (the malignancy ratings file) is read from
  `data/LNDb/data/chars_trainNodules.csv`, which does NOT exist in this checkout — the full
  765-nodule label set is not reproducible here without that file (only the 659-nodule
  `notebooks/lndb_processed.csv` is available).
- The test CTs (testdata0/testdata1) are not extracted in this checkout either, so the
  submission step cannot be re-run here as-is.
- All code is written to be re-runnable and heavily cross-referenced between parts;
  references are collected at the end of each part (IBSI/Zwanenburg 2020, PyRadiomics/
  van Griethuysen 2017, Shafiq-ul-Hassan 2017, Traverso 2018, Koo & Li 2016, etc.).
